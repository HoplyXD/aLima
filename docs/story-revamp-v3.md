## 1. Nang Shine (Auntie) — the photo chain

**Window: 12:00–16:00, shop door.** Completion is always **outside: hand the cleaned
piece to Auntie, who stands beside Alya at the yard gate.**

| Quest | Day | Piece | Completion prize |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **Day 1** (fallback **Day 3** if missed) | Faded photo — **the Artisan as a child** | **Unlocks Artisan visits** the next day (D2, or D4 on the fallback) |
| Q2 | **Day 3** | Her photo frame | She promises to return Day 5 |
| Q3 | **Day 5** (window opens early: **09:00–16:00**) | Photo — **young Shine & young Yuyu** | Her thanks + the **Safe code is visible in the photo** |

**The Safe.** A safe sits **outside, at the scrapyard** (new interactable). Interacting
without the code plays a player monologue; with Q3 done in a **previous** loop, it opens:
**₱1,000 + 1 fragment** (a bare find — Found → Portal → seat).

**Dialogue drafts:**

- *Q1 intro (door):* "Ay, good noon, child. I found this in the wardrobe — a little boy,
  all knees and sawdust. Clean it for me? You'll know him when you see him, ha."
- *Q1 hand-off (gate):* "There he is. My grandson — Lave. He works with wood now, like
  he promised that day. Go see him tomorrow, child. Tell him his Lola sent you."
- *Q2 intro:* "The frame this time. The glass has gone foggy — like my eyes, ha. Don't
  laugh."
- *Q2 hand-off:* "Clear as rain. Friday, child. Friday I'll bring you the last one. It's…
  it's the one I should have brought first."
- *Q3 intro:* "Here. Before you clean it, promise me you won't ask who the boy is. You'll
  know anyway. Everyone knew, once."
- *Q3 hand-off:* "…That's us. Him and me, before your Lola ever minded the shop. [i]She
  presses the photo back into your hands.[/i] Look at the porch rail, child. He always
  said numbers keep better than promises." **[The Safe code is worked into the carving
  in the photo.]**
- *Safe monologue (no code):* "Heavy, old, and locked. Four dials. Yuyu never wrote
  anything down… except when he did. Somebody must remember the number."
- *Safe opening (code known):* "The rail from the photo — the same numbers. [i]The door
  swings open like it was waiting.[/i]"

## 3. Alya (Scavenger) — the bag chain

**Available at the yard, Days 1–4.**

| Quest | Day | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Day 1+ | Talk to Alya → she hands her **lunch box** to clean. Done: you ask about Yuyu — she remembers him **meeting a girl**, then saying he was going to **her old house (the Dump Site)** for "the final piece." |
| Q2 | Days 2–4 | **Unlocks the Dump Site (next loops).** Her story: adopted by Yuyu after her father — Yuyu's best friend — passed. In the Dump Site you find evidence of Yuyu; **her sling bag spawns at a random Dump Site spot** (only after Q1). Found: she tells why she became a scavenger (the bag was her father's last gift, and she lost it), and **begs you to clean it**. |
| Q3 | Day 5 | Q2 done → **Forbidden Zone opens (Day 5)**. A **salakot spawns at a random Forbidden Zone spot**. Clean it → **one buyer always appears for it: Sam.** Selling it to her **unlocks Sam's place (next loops)**. After meeting Sam, Alya realizes **Sam is the girl** Yuyu met — and remembers a **golden fragment inside her bag**; she gives it to you. *(The bag is a carrier: clean → open, §4-D ✓.)* |

**Dialogue drafts:**

- *Q1 intro:* "Oy! Before you ask — yes, I eat. This lunch box, though… it stopped being
  for food a long time ago. Clean it? Careful with the lid, ha."
- *Q1 done / Yuyu question:* "Tito? Last I saw him he'd been talking with some girl —
  serious type, notebook. Then he says to me, 'Alya, I'm going to your old house. The
  final piece is there.' My old house is a dump now. Literally, ha."
- *Bag found (Dump Site):* "…Where did you— [i]she takes it with both hands and forgets
  to be loud.[/i] Tatay gave me this. I lost it the year I lost him. I've been digging
  through other people's garbage for years just in case it… please. Clean it. Please."
- *Q3, after meeting Sam:* "THAT'S her! The notebook girl! …Wait. Waaait. The bag —
  there was something heavy sewn under the lining, I thought it was a sinker. [i]She
  shoves the bag at you.[/i] Open it. Open it open it open it."

## 4. Mysterious Buyer — the overpriced shop (economy inverted: he SELLS)

**Visits the shop daily, 18:00–20:00** *(user spec said 6–10pm; the shop day latches
closed at 20:00 — see flag F2)*. Each day he offers **one overpriced artifact**. Buy all
five (one per day) → on Day 5 he also offers **the fragment for ₱5,000**.

| Day | Price | Rarity (glow legend) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₱300–700 | white–green |
| 2 | ₱300–700 | white–blue |
| 3 | ₱900–1,100 | green–blue |
| 4 | ₱1,400–1,600 | blue–purple |
| 5 | ₱1,400–1,600 | purple |
| 5 (bonus) | **₱5,000** | **gold — the fragment** |

*(User spec used "epic"; mapped to the fixed glow legend §4-E: rare→blue, epic→purple.)*

- *Daily patter:* "Good evening. I sell exactly one thing a day, and it is always
  overpriced. The trick, kid, is that some days it's worth it."
- *Day-5 fragment offer:* "You bought all five. Sentiment? Strategy? Either way — you've
  proven you pay for what matters. So: the real inventory. [i]He sets a small cloth
  bundle on the counter.[/i] Five thousand. I promised a man I'd only sell it to someone
  who'd already paid attention four times."
